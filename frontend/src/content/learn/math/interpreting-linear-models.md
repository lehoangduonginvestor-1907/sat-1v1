Many SAT questions give a linear equation that models a real situation and ask what one of its numbers **means**. You don't need to solve anything; you need to translate between the algebra and the story.

## Slope = rate, intercept = starting value

In a model $y = mx + b$:

:::rule Meaning of the parts
- The **slope $m$** is the change in $y$ for each 1-unit increase in $x$: a rate (dollars per hour, degrees per minute, pages per day).
- The **$y$-intercept $b$** is the value of $y$ when $x = 0$: a starting amount, fixed fee, or initial value.
:::

:::examples
✓ A plumber charges $C = 65h + 40$ dollars for $h$ hours of work. The 65 means the cost increases by \$65 for each additional hour; the 40 is a fixed charge (the cost when $h = 0$).
✓ The height of a candle is $H = 18 - 1.5t$ centimeters after $t$ hours. The candle starts at 18 cm and gets 1.5 cm shorter each hour.
:::

A negative slope means the quantity **decreases** as $x$ increases.

## Read the units

The units of the slope are always "$y$-units per $x$-unit." Use them to check your interpretation. In $W = 0.8d + 150$, where $W$ is a weight in grams and $d$ is a number of days, 0.8 is "grams per day."

:::trap Watch what the variable counts
If $x$ is the number of **years since 2010**, then $x = 0$ means the year 2010, not year 0. If $x$ counts hundreds of items, a slope of 3 means 3 per **hundred** items. Always reread the definition of each variable.
:::

## Equations in standard form

Some models are written as $Ax + By = C$, often a budget or a total: "Ana spends \$12 per notebook and \$5 per pen and spends \$120 in all": $12n + 5p = 120$.

- Each coefficient is a price or rate for one item.
- The constant on the right is the total.
- To interpret something like "the $n$-intercept," set $p = 0$: $n = 10$, the number of notebooks she could buy if she bought no pens.

Units make the coefficients easy to identify: every term must have the same units as the total. If $4c + 9s = 300$ is a total in dollars and $s$ counts shirts, then $9s$ is in dollars, so 9 must be **dollars per shirt**. And dividing the total by an intercept gives a price: buying only shirts, $s = \tfrac{300}{9}$, so each shirt costs $\tfrac{300}{300/9} = 9$ dollars.

## Flipping and scaling the rate

- The slope answers "how much does $y$ change when $x$ goes up by 1?" To answer "how much must $x$ increase for $y$ to go up by 1?", use the **reciprocal** of the slope. If height grows $\tfrac{2}{5}$ inch per gram of fertilizer, it takes $\tfrac{5}{2} = 2.5$ grams to add one inch.
- For several units of $x$, multiply: a drop of 3°F per minute means a drop of 15°F over 5 minutes. Don't report the per-unit rate when the question asks about a longer interval.

## Interpreting other numbers

Questions may ask about a combination of terms, such as "what does $65h$ represent?" (the hourly portion of the total cost) or "what is the meaning of the point $(3, 235)$ on the graph?" (after 3 hours the cost is \$235). Translate each part using the variable definitions.

:::steps
1. Underline what each variable represents, including units.
2. Identify the part being asked about: slope, intercept, a term, or a point.
3. Say what changes, by how much, per unit of what. Then match a choice.
:::

:::check
The number of liters of water in a tank $t$ minutes after it begins draining is modeled by $W = 900 - 25t$. What is the best interpretation of 25 in this context?
- [ ] The tank initially holds 25 liters of water.
- [ ] The tank is empty after 25 minutes.
- [x] The amount of water in the tank decreases by 25 liters each minute.
- [ ] The amount of water in the tank decreases by 25 percent each minute.
> 25 is the slope's magnitude: liters per minute, and the minus sign means a decrease. A linear model never means a percent change.
:::

:::check
A gym's monthly cost is $C = 30m + 50$, where $m$ is the number of months a member has belonged. What does 50 represent?
- [ ] the cost per month
- [x] a one-time cost, such as a sign-up fee
- [ ] the total cost after one month
- [ ] the number of months needed to reach \$50
> 50 is the value of $C$ when $m = 0$: a fixed charge that doesn't depend on months. The total after one month would be $30 + 50 = 80$.
:::

## Summary

- Slope = rate of change (y-units per x-unit); intercept = value when $x = 0$.
- Use the units and variable definitions to check every interpretation.
- In $Ax + By = C$, coefficients are per-item amounts and $C$ is the total.
- A linear model describes a constant amount of change, never a constant percent.
