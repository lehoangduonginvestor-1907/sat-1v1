Ratios, Rates, Proportional Relationships, and Units is a Problem-Solving and Data Analysis skill. The questions reward careful bookkeeping more than clever math. The key tool is the **conversion factor**.

## Conversion factors

A conversion factor is a fraction equal to 1, like $\dfrac{60 \text{ min}}{1 \text{ hr}}$. Multiply by factors arranged so that unwanted units cancel.

$$45 \,\frac{\text{mi}}{\text{hr}} \times \frac{1 \text{ hr}}{60 \text{ min}} \times \frac{5{,}280 \text{ ft}}{1 \text{ mi}} = 3{,}960 \,\frac{\text{ft}}{\text{min}}$$

:::steps
1. Write the starting quantity with its units.
2. Multiply by a factor that puts the unit you want to remove in the **opposite** position (top ↔ bottom).
3. Repeat until only the target units remain, then compute.
:::

## Converting units of area and volume

Squared and cubed units need the conversion applied twice or three times.

- $1 \text{ ft} = 12 \text{ in}$, so $1 \text{ ft}^2 = 12^2 = 144 \text{ in}^2$ and $1 \text{ ft}^3 = 12^3 = 1{,}728 \text{ in}^3$.
- $1 \text{ m}^2 = 100^2 = 10{,}000 \text{ cm}^2$.

:::trap The square-unit trap
Converting 3 square yards to square feet: $3 \times 3^2 = 27$, not $3 \times 3 = 9$. One yard is 3 feet, so one **square** yard is $3 \times 3 = 9$ square feet.
:::

## Speed, rate, and work

$$\text{distance} = \text{rate} \times \text{time} \qquad \text{amount} = \text{rate} \times \text{time}$$

For **average speed** over a whole trip, divide total distance by total time. Don't average the speeds: driving 60 mph one way and 40 mph back over the same distance gives an average of 48 mph, not 50.

## Average rate of change

The average rate of change of a quantity between two points is the slope between them:

$$\frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}$$

On a graph or table, pick the two points and compute change in output over change in input, with units (e.g., "degrees per hour").

## Ratios and proportions

- A ratio $a : b$ compares parts. If boys : girls $= 3 : 5$, then boys make up $\tfrac{3}{8}$ of the group. The **total** has $3 + 5 = 8$ parts.
- To split a total in a given ratio, find the value of one part: 64 students in a $3:5$ ratio → 1 part $= 8$, so 24 boys and 40 girls.
- A **proportion** sets two ratios equal; solve by cross-multiplying: $\dfrac{3}{7} = \dfrac{x}{56} \Rightarrow x = 24$.
- Two quantities are **directly proportional** if $y = kx$ for a constant $k$ (doubling one doubles the other).
- **Linking two ratios** that share a quantity: if flour : sugar $= 3 : 2$ and sugar : butter $= 5 : 4$, rescale so sugar matches (the LCM of 2 and 5 is 10): flour : sugar $= 15 : 10$ and sugar : butter $= 10 : 8$, so flour : sugar : butter $= 15 : 10 : 8$.

:::tip Scale drawings and maps
If 1 inch on a map represents 25 miles, set up $\dfrac{1 \text{ in}}{25 \text{ mi}} = \dfrac{x \text{ in}}{\text{distance}}$ with the units in matching positions.
:::

- [ ] 450

- [ ] 4,320
> 2.5 hours = 150 minutes, and $18 \times 150 = 2{,}700$.

- [ ] 18
- [ ] 24
- [x] 54
- [ ] 162
> 1 square yard = $3^2 = 9$ square feet, so $6 \times 9 = 54$.
:::

:::check
The ratio of red marbles to blue marbles in a bag is $2 : 7$. If there are 63 marbles in the bag, how many are red?
- [ ] 9
- [x] 14
- [ ] 18
- [ ] 49
> There are $2 + 7 = 9$ parts, so each part is $63 \div 9 = 7$ marbles. Red: $2 \times 7 = 14$.
:::

## Summary

- Chain conversion factors so units cancel.
- Square and cube the conversion for area and volume.
- Average speed = total distance ÷ total time.
- Ratios: count total parts, find one part, scale up. Proportions: cross-multiply.
